For years I've kept notes in scratch files: how a specific SageMaker
endpoint kept warming twice, why a Terraform module needed a null_resource
to unblock, what actually goes wrong when you point a RAG pipeline at
messy PDFs.

Rather than lose those to my Downloads folder, I'm going to write them
here. Short posts. Real problems, real fixes. If any of it helps another
MLOps engineer avoid the same afternoon I lost, great.

Topics I'll be writing about:

- Production ML deployments (AWS SageMaker, EKS, Terraform)
- GenAI / RAG systems that don't fall over in production
- Observability for AI workloads (DataDog, OpenTelemetry)
- Small, sharp tools
