About Me

Empowering AI solutions from prototype to production

Results-driven MLOps Engineer with 7 years of software engineering experience and 3+ years specializing in production ML systems, GenAI platforms, and cloud infrastructure. My mission is to drive technological innovation by building robust, scalable AI solutions that deliver real-world impact for enterprises.

As an Intermediate MLOps Engineer at Clio and former Technical Lead (AI Solutions Architect) at CitiusTech, I've architected and deployed scalable ML pipelines, RAG-enabled AI features, and multi-region GenAI platforms across healthcare and legal domains. My expertise includes AWS (SageMaker, EKS), Azure OpenAI, Terraform, Kubernetes, DataDog, OpenTelemetry, and integrating LLMs within production infrastructure.

Delivered projects that reduced ML deployment times by 40%, optimized cloud infrastructure by 50%, and enabled observability and compliance for enterprise AI systems.

Specialized in designing end-to-end solutions: from real-time ML workflows in healthcare and legal tech, to comprehensive monitoring and robust security for global deployments.

Skilled in translating ambiguous requirements into production-ready systems, leveraging T-shaped skills—high-level architecture vision combined with deep technical execution.

My academic foundation includes an MS in Computer Science from Liverpool John Moores University, UK, and a PG degree from IIIT Bangalore. I hold certifications in AWS Solutions Architecture, Data Science, and more. Driven by continuous learning, cross-team collaboration, and a hands-on approach, I constantly explore new technologies to solve complex business challenges.

If you're seeking an AI engineer who can bridge business needs and technical execution, and who's passionate about impactful, innovative solutions—let's connect!
